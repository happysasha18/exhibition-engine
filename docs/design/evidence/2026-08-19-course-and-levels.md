## 2. The cue's own course

Every driven handle used to author its own shape and its own middle. A cue driving twelve handles
therefore carried twelve journeys that started and finished together and were free to disagree
everywhere between, which is the opposite of what the grammar's fifth law asks — properties that
belong to one gesture hang on one scalar so they cannot disagree.

A cue now writes ONE course, `<cue>-course`, and every travelling handle reads it through a `map`
onto its own two measured ends. The course is normalised: nought is the departing work's own reading
of a handle and one is the arriving work's. Direction, span and provenance stay the handle's own;
only the shape is shared. Where the course would carry a reading past what an instrument publishes,
the map stands inside the client's own `clamp`, so a course shared by twelve handles cannot push any
one of them past its own manifest.

The course carries the room of the middle and it can HOLD it. `reach` — the two works' own tone
apartness, the same reading the door and the witness camera already take on this pair — carries the
course past one in the direction of travel, and the same scalar says how long the room is held: the
hold takes `reach` of the shorter leg, so two works far apart in tone stand still in the middle for
longer and two standing close barely pause. The bound is the shorter leg itself, so both legs keep a
real journey however far apart the works stand.

A held pair of points is a genuine dwell on this road rather than a step, and the reason is the
spline this road already draws: `splineSlopes` sets both tangents of a flat segment to zero, so the
course arrives at the room with no speed left and leaves it the same way. The movement is continuous
in value and in speed across the whole passage.

The weight fence needed nothing new. `fitTheWeight`'s last rung sheds a spline to a two-point `mix`
over its own first and last points; a course's first and last points are nought and one, so a shed
course becomes a plain eased nought-to-one and every reader goes on mapping it onto its own ends.
The shape is what is lost, which is the rung it was always meant to be.

## 3. The levels law

**In the cast.** The morning's relaxation lets a candidate stand on an occupied level whenever it
owns one level of its own, and rests that on the promise that a non-owner's handles for the lost
level are held at the instrument's rest. The promise is kept on LIGHT-COLOUR alone, through
`singsLightColour`; everywhere else a non-owner writes its own pattern handles and draws them. The
test is therefore narrowed to where the promise is kept: a candidate is excluded when every level it
declares is already claimed, as before, AND when it would stand beside another VOICE on an occupied
level that carries no pinning gate of its own. A level joins `PINNED_LEVELS` the day a gate exists
for it, which keeps the exemption earned by a mechanism rather than open by assertion.

The ground stands aside from that second test and its own clash record says so. It holds the whole
passage by construction, so counting it as a competitor is not strict but unsatisfiable — measured
this afternoon, when the strict form left `adrift` and `gears` uncastable at every slot on almost
every pair because the ground holds SURFACE from the first frame to the last. The first test still
counts the ground: a candidate with nothing free to say at all is still excluded.

**At the instant — built, measured, and taken out again.** A guard was added to `playFrame`: each
live voice claims the levels its cue declares, and a voice declaring a level already claimed by a
voice beneath it does not draw. It read correctly on its own suite and it was wrong, and the tree
had already written down why.

`cue.levels` is what a cue STANDS ON, not what it OWNS. A cue accompanying on a level it does not
drive declares that level exactly as its owner does, and no field on the wire tells the two apart —
`levelOwnership` is stripped before a score ships, because §4.4's cue allow-list is closed and a
score is refused whole on any unknown field. So the guard silenced legitimate accompaniment: three
suites went red, `pass_stack`, `pass_adrift` and `pass_coverage`, each on a hand-authored score whose
three cues share SURFACE while testing the stack, the coverage and the one-canvas law. `pass_stack`'s
own row 4 states the contract in as many words — the host takes a composed passage without a word
about levels, because the law is decidable from the authored plan and nowhere else.

That is the same shape as the afternoon's finding: a levels test that counts every declared level as
a claim is not strict, it is unsatisfiable. The guard is out, and nothing of it is left standing.

**What answers his ask anyway, and it landed this morning.** The relabel he named is gone from the
wire already: `ownTheLevels` strikes a lost level out of the cue's own `levels` before a score is
written, so a losing voice does not carry that level at all — it left it. What remains is that a
non-owner still drives the handles belonging to that level, and that is the per-handle declaration
named in section 4, not a runtime check.

### The red proofs

The cast: over forty composed passages on named pairs, no two voices above the floor share a level
across meeting windows. With the repair reverted in a copy of the composer alone, the same forty
passages carry two — `gears` with `grid-colour` on CELL, and the material instrument with `adrift`
on SURFACE. `test_pass_composed.py` reads 66 of 66 with the law in place, the same as without it,
and the cast it prints redistributes rather than thins: no instrument leaves the field.

A false diagnosis on the way, recorded because it nearly cost the law. The first run with the cast
law read red on the row proving the gates instrument reads its slot off the departing work, and the
law was withdrawn for it. The cause was elsewhere entirely — the `clamp` wrapper described above had
taken the provenance note one level down, and that row skips any node whose note does not open with
«requested», so ten of the gates readings vanished from a row that had nothing to do with levels.
With the note on the outermost node the row reads its full 60 readings and four distinct widths
again, with the cast law standing. A cause that fires on one row and spares the twenty around it is
worth doubting before a repair is given up for it.

The runtime guard had its own suite and its own red proof, and both are gone with it. Worth
recording that the proof was sound and the guard was still wrong: a mutation that reddens a row
proves the row can see the mechanism, and says nothing about whether the mechanism belongs there.

## 4. What this does NOT close, said plainly

The collisions that remain are between the GROUND and a voice above it. The cast law closes the
collisions between voices above the floor and leaves those, because the ground occupies its levels
from the first frame to the last and nothing can share with it until it can hand the floor over.

Two roads close them and neither is started here. Shelf 3's enfilade — the ground hands the floor
over before the passage ends — which is the capability his letter already names as next. Or a
declaration, per handle, of which structural level that handle drives, in each instrument's own
manifest: the one thing that would let a non-owner rest on the level it lost while going on playing
the level it owns, which is all `singsLightColour` is, generalised.

And the frame loop stays silent about levels, which is the tree's own settled contract rather than
a gap. The law is decidable from the authored plan and from nothing else, so it is enforced where
the plan is authored — in the cast, where tonight's work strengthened it and where its red proof
lives.
