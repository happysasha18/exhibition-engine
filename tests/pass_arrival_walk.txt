WHAT REACHING A HANDLE MEANS. `pour.arrival`/`pour.seedPlace` order the pour's
own columns outward from the seed; `livemirror.propagate` spreads the mirrored
copies' exchanges apart, the further copy first; `overlay.arrival` and
`grid-colour.arrival` carry the interfered arrival. CARRIED and CONDENSED have no
handle of their own: the first is the gesture already running and the second is
the arrival instrument's own cast, which the `cast:` line above names.
