S-19 - the letter cooldown's floor, before and after 2026-08-26
========================================================================

weight = fit * cooldown(at, poolSize); cooldown = 1 if at < 0 (never played),
else (at+1)/(poolSize+1).
BEFORE: poolSize = the walk's raw log length (grows without end as a visit runs).
AFTER:  poolSize = the walk's DISTINCT letters (bounded by the vocabulary in play).

-- 1. THE FLOOR ITSELF, AS THE SAME ROAD KEEPS BEING PLAYED ------------
The pair's own favourite road, played on every passage of the visit so far (the exact
failure walkPlayed's own comment names: 'a letter that suits a whole collection well
carried step after step of one route'). Column shown: what fraction of its own fit that
road's weight keeps, played most recently, as the visit lengthens.

The 'before' column has no floor: it halves roughly every doubling of the visit and
keeps falling for as long as the visit runs. The 'after' column is exactly 0.5 at every
length, because one road played any number of times is still ONE distinct letter.

-- 2. WHEN A NEVER-PLAYED RIVAL OVERTAKES THE FAVOURITE ----------------
Same walk (the pair's own best-fit road, played on every passage so far) against a rival
of fixed, lower fit that the visit has simply never played (cooldown neutral, weight =
its own fit throughout — the ordinary case for a road no walk has touched). 'crossover'
is the first visit length at which the rival's fixed weight overtakes the favourite's.
